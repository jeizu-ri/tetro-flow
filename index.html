How to play

Battle 2P, Tetris Friends style: Hold on the left, Next on the right, 10×20 wells. SRS rotations, guideline scoring (T-Spin Double is 1200 × level, back-to-back ×1.5). Level up every 10 lines — gravity and points both climb. Line clears send garbage (doubles, triples, Tetrises, T-spins, back-to-back, combos). Incoming garbage can be cancelled by attacking. First to 3 KOs wins. If 2:00 runs out, KOs, then garbage sent, then stack height decide it.
